Four steps, one finished record

Everything below already works in the app above — record it, share it, publish it.

01 · CAPTURE
Record the idea
Live mic capture, a full multitrack mixer, and stems that stay separate all the way to release.
02 · COLLABORATE
Invite the finishers
Send a project, not a file. Collaborators add stems, comment in time, and message in the same place.
03 · SETTLE
Agree the splits
Percentages must total 100 and every party must sign before the publish button unlocks. No post-hoc disputes.
04 · RELEASE
Publish and rank
Five tags put the track on five charts. Spikes move it. Listeners find it by scene, not by algorithm.

Provenance is the product

When a song takes nine seconds to generate, the scarce thing is proof that people made it together. Ellipsis records that proof as the track is built, not after it is uploaded.
