When anyone can generate a song in nine seconds, the scarce thing is proof that people made it — together.
So we rank verified human collaborations across five small charts instead of one impossible one.

WHY NOW
Four things became true in the last eighteen months
Provenance became a product
Platforms now tag, demonetise and delist synthetic uploads. Being able to prove a human made something is a feature people will pick a platform for.
The industry is hunting fraud
IFPI's 2026 report calls for action on streaming fraud at every stage of the value chain. A chart that cannot be farmed is now commercially interesting, not just tidy.
Growth moved to our markets
Latin America grew 17.1%, MENA and Sub-Saharan Africa 15.2%. The fastest-growing scenes are exactly the ones without local collaboration infrastructure.
Production went phone-first
A current phone records, mixes and exports broadcast-quality stems. The studio is no longer the bottleneck — trust between strangers is.

DEFENSIBILITY
Proof is the product, not the paperwork
Humans only
A rhythm check plus device attestation gates publishing, Spiking and messaging. In a year when most uploads elsewhere are synthetic and most of their plays are bots, a chart that cannot be farmed is a saleable asset.
Switching cost: signal quality
Fingerprint on save
Every stem hashed and matched against commercial catalogue and our own. Registry ID, timestamp, inaudible watermark, AI-training opt-out — issued before the track is public.
Switching cost: provenance
Splits before sound
Signed by every party before publish. A contributor keeps their share even after they lose access — the product says so in plain language, because that is how music rights actually work.

RISKS WE ARE NOT HIDING
Four ways this gets hard
Creation funnels leak
Most signups never finish a track. Mitigation: the listener side monetises from day one, and a Spike is a complete action that costs nothing to perform.
BandLab has scale already
They own the free social DAW. We are not fighting for that — we win on ranked discovery and settled rights, which they have no reason to build.
Trust and safety is a real cost
UGC audio and artwork means classifiers, human review and DMCA operations from launch. Budgeted at 12% of the seed, not discovered later.
The name is unsearchable
“Ellipsis” collides with grammar results and several products. Trademark and ASO review before any brand spend — better to rename now than at scale.
