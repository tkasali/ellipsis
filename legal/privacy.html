Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026 · RoundTable Ventures, LLC

What we collect

Account details you give us (name, email, handle, profile image); content you create (recordings, stems, mixes, artwork, messages, split agreements); and technical data needed to run the service (device model, OS version, app version, IP address, crash traces).

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you are actively recording in the app. Audio is never captured in the background, and nothing is transmitted until you save or share a take.

Why we process it

  • To deliver the service — hosting your projects, syncing collaborators, publishing releases.
  • To verify that content is human-made — liveness checks, device attestation and audio fingerprinting.
  • To administer rights — retaining signed split agreements and handling copyright claims.
  • To keep the platform safe — moderation of uploaded audio and images, abuse and fraud prevention.
  • To bill subscriptions, processed by Apple and Google; we never see your card details.

Sharing

We share data with processors acting on our instructions: cloud hosting and CDN, audio fingerprinting, image moderation, analytics and crash reporting, and payment platforms. We do not sell personal data and we do not use your recordings to train generative models.

Retention

Content is kept while your account is open. Rights records — split agreements, release metadata and takedown correspondence — are retained for seven years after a release, because they are legal records. Everything else is deleted within 30 days of account deletion.

Your choices

You can delete your account from Settings inside the app, or at https://ellipsismusic.net/delete-account. You may request access, correction, export or erasure at tosin.kasali@gmail.com. If you are in the EEA or UK, our lawful bases are contract, legitimate interests and consent for the microphone; you may complain to your supervisory authority. If you are in California, you have the rights described in the CCPA/CPRA and we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined.

Children

Ellipsis is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA). Accounts found to belong to children are removed.
